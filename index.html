Shop Pulse
Early Stage • In Development

Modernizing estimating for manufacturers.

Shop Pulse is an AI-assisted estimating tool currently in early development. We're exploring how intelligent systems can plug into existing ERPs to help machine shops respond to RFQs faster and with greater confidence.

What we're building

Shop Pulse aims to support estimators by:

  • • Drafting AI-assisted quote suggestions using historical job data
  • • Providing transparent cost assumptions (materials, cycle time, etc.)
  • • Helping generate clear client communication around pricing
  • • Integrating into existing ERP systems rather than replacing them

Our mission

Manufacturing runs on precision, yet quoting often remains manual and time-consuming. Our mission is to explore how AI can augment estimators — not replace them — bringing speed, clarity, and better decision-making into the quoting process.

Current status

Shop Pulse is in its early stages. We are currently researching workflows, validating problems with manufacturers, and designing the first iteration of the product.
